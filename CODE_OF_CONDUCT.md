# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to make participation in Architectural Prompt Kit a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.
